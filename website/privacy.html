Privacy

Timelane collects no data. There is no account, no analytics and no advertising.

Everything you record, the apps you track and the hours you log, is stored locally on your Mac, inside the app's sandbox.

If you turn on the optional iCloud backup (off by default), a copy of your data is stored in your own private iCloud database. Only you can access it; the developer of Timelane cannot read it.

This website uses no cookies, no analytics and no external resources.
